# Shadow norm from G 2026-08-17

Use this before the next heading tweak.

- Big cream titles go heavy fast. Start light.
- Smaller lines and taglines go flat fast. Give them more drop than the big title.
- Dark orange / color-3 lines look heavier than they are. Give them LESS shadow.
- Kickers need a real drop. Do not strip them.
- Never use the 5px four-stop black stack.
- "A little" means a small step, not a flip.
- Scrolls / Patio lock = tight hard 2-stop, not smear.
- One screenshot = only those lines.
